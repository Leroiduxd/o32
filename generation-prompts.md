Three-quarter close-up, natural soft side light, warm appetizing colours, realistic affordable French snack portions, no luxury fine dining. Landscape composition, sandwich clearly legible with space around it. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no menu layout. This is a completely new illustrative photograph, not a copy of any attached menu or its photographs.
